# General

The Orders & Observations (OO) Incubator Implementation Guide serves as a consolidated workspace for developing and evaluating emerging specifications, including FHIR resources, profiles, operations, vocabularies, and artifacts developed by the HL7 Orders & Observations Work Group. It includes resources that are actively evolving and are non-normative under the FHIR Maturity Model. These artifacts reflect the current direction of ongoing work but should not be interpreted as stable normative specifications.

Published versions of these artifacts may be used in implementations, including production deployments, but implementers should do so with the understanding that:

- Artifacts may change significantly as they progress through the FHIR Maturity Model.
- Backward compatibility is not guaranteed.
- Items may be promoted into official HL7 balloted specifications, or removed if superseded or no longer aligned with Work Group direction.
- Implementer feedback is a critical input and may directly influence future revisions.

This IG defines the scope of exploration for the Orders & Observations Work Group and provides early visibility into emerging content. Implementers are encouraged to assess their risk tolerance, track updates regularly, and participate in the standards development process where possible.
